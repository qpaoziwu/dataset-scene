    The objects and their fine detail maps are generated in the browser. Tabletop textures are Poly Haven scans; the phones are a supplied glTF model with its own materials. Boxes are computed from each object's 3D box corners, so they run slightly looser than mask-based COCO boxes. The path tracer ignores displacement and screen-space effects; it computes shadows, occlusion and reflections directly.
